How we work

Specification, sample, documentation

Production is calibrated around a material behaving the same way every time. Granulation and moisture that drift between lots show up as yield problems, and they rarely get traced back to the ingredient.

So everything starts with a written specification and a sample. Both sides agree what is being bought before anything is produced, which removes most of the disputes this trade generates.

Documentation does the rest. Lot coding and paperwork that travels with the shipment mean a question months later has an answer, which matters to any buyer with a compliance obligation of their own.
