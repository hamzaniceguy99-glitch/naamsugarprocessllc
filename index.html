How we work

A Florida company, business to business

NAAM Sugar Process LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. We supply commercial buyers rather than consumers.

We make no nutritional or health claims about sugar, and we don't advise on your labelling or regulatory obligations — those are yours, and they depend on your product and your market. Specifications, certificates of analysis and facility documentation are provided on request so your own compliance team has what it needs.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you sell to the public?

No. We supply businesses: manufacturers, bakeries, packers and distributors. Orders are quoted against a written specification.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes, and we'd encourage it. Samples and the specification sheet go out before any commitment on either side.

How is pricing set?

Per lot, in writing. Sugar is a commodity and prices move, so standing agreements state plainly how price is handled over time.

What documentation comes with an order?

Specification and lot documentation with each shipment, and certificates of analysis or facility documentation on request.

Do you handle export shipments?

We arrange freight and prepare commercial documentation. Customs entries are filed by your licensed broker, not by us.

What are your lead times?

They depend on grade, volume and format. We quote a realistic lead time in writing rather than an optimistic one.

Need a quote on a grade or a volume?

Send the specification and the volumes. We reply within one business day.
